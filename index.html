Dr. Sachin Mishra

Principal Investigator, Materials Science

I develop biosensors and the bio-interfaces behind them, and reinforce hydrogel matrices with cellulose nanocrystals. My work combines polymer and nanocomposite formulation with nanoscale characterization by atomic force microscopy and related techniques.

Research

  • Cellulose nanocrystal-reinforced hydrogels

    Nanocomposite matrices tuned for mechanical strength, optical clarity and interfacial behaviour.

  • Functional bio-interfaces

    Surface chemistries that switch on and off, connecting biological recognition to an electronic signal.

  • Biosensor development

    Label-free, real-time sensing chips, from graphene oxide capacitive devices to nanoporous metasurfaces.

  • Nanoscale characterization

    AFM in liquid, force spectroscopy and magnetic force microscopy to measure morphology, surface potential and mechanics.
